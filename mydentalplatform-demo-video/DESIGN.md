## Style Prompt
Create a professional dental SaaS demo video for MyDentalPlatform: calm clinical confidence, clean product surfaces, trustworthy dental blues, soft mint highlights, and mobile-first SaaS interface motion. The video should feel like a premium clinic operations product, not a loud startup ad.

## Colors
- Clinical navy: `#0B2E4A` for authority and primary text.
- Dental blue: `#1D64D8` for CTA and product highlights.
- Mint care: `#7AE2C7` for health/availability accents.
- Warm white: `#F7FBFF` for clean clinic surfaces.
- Slate ink: `#273445` for supporting copy.

## Typography
- Display: `Newsreader`, high-contrast editorial trust for major claims.
- Body/UI: `DM Sans`, clear SaaS product readability.
- Data labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, precise operational details.

## Motion
- Use soft focus pulls, push slides, staggered cards, and light clinical glows.
- Keep transitions smooth and medium-energy.
- Every scene should enter with motion; avoid jump cuts.

## What NOT To Do
- Do not use neon, purple startup gradients, or generic tech-dark styling.
- Do not use Inter, Roboto, Poppins, or default system typography.
- Do not overcrowd screens; show one idea per scene.
- Do not make medical treatment claims or guaranteed growth claims.
